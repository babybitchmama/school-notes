\chapter*{Summary}
\label{ch:summary}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{\nameref{ch:summary}}

In 1981, Berard Bergery published a paper titled `Scalar Curvature and Isometry Group' in which he proved that a manifold $M$ admits a $g$-invariant metric with positive scalar curvature if and only if the quotient $M/G$ admits a metric with positive scalar curvature, where here, $G$ is a compact Lie group acting on $M$ by isometries.
