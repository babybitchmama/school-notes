\chapter{The Boundary Distance Rigidity Problem}
\label{chap:2}

Now that we've developed the necessary geometric tools required, we're now able to start looking at some interesting problems. Throughout this chapter, we'll only be looking at one specific problem, the Boundary Distance Rigidity Problem, and its applications to medical imaging. This problem is a fundamental question in the field of geometric inverse problems, and it has many applications in various fields, including medical imaging, geophysics, and computer vision.
