  % 2. What are the real or potential impacts of academic integrity? How does
  %    this affect you, other students, the institution?
  Academic integrity is important because it ensures that students are
  evaluated based on their own work and abilities, rather than evaluating work
  based on other students without proper attribution. It affects the student as
  it hinders their learning and development, as they are not engaging with the
  material in a meaningful way. It also leads to a lack of understanding of the
  subject matter, which can have long-term consequences for their academic and
  professional careers. It affects other students as it creates an unfair
  advantage for those who follow the rules and work hard to earn their grades.
  It can also create a culture of dishonesty and mistrust among students and
  faculty, which can negatively impact the learning environment. It also
  affects the curve that the professor may give, as if some students use unfair
  means to get a better grade, it can lower the grades of other students who
  are following the rules. It affects the institution as it undermines the
  credibility of the degrees it awards and can lead to a loss of trust from
  employers and the public. It can also result in legal and financial
  consequences for the institution if academic integrity violations are not
  properly addressed.
